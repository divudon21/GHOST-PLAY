## Overview

**GHOST PLAY** is a modern, high‑performance media player for Android that handles
virtually any video or audio format. Powered by Media3 (ExoPlayer) with an FFmpeg
software decoder fallback, it delivers smooth playback, rich gesture controls, and a
clean, minimal interface that adapts to your taste.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎥 Video Playback
- **Universal format support** — MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WebM, FLV, TS and more, with HLS,
  DASH and RTSP streaming.
- **Hardware & software decoding** — automatic hardware acceleration with a bundled
  FFmpeg decoder fallback for unsupported codecs.
- **Picture‑in‑Picture** — keep watching in a floating window while using other apps.
- **Resume playback** — pick up exactly where you left off.
- **Adjustable playback speed** — fine control from 0.25× to 3.0×.
- **Flexible screen orientation** — auto, portrait, landscape, or sensor‑based.

### 🎧 Audio Playback
- **Full‑screen Now Playing experience** — large album art, track details, and a
  scrubbable seek bar.
- **Optimised audio library loading** — tracks are loaded progressively to keep large
  collections smooth and responsive.
- **Mini player** — a compact control bar that follows you across the app; tap or
  swipe up to expand into the full player.
- **Background playback** — keep listening with the screen off.
- **Media notification & lock‑screen controls** — play, pause, and skip from anywhere.
- **Album art & metadata** — embedded artwork and artist details are displayed
  automatically.
- **Volume Boost** — amplify quiet tracks up to 200%.

### 👆 Gesture Controls
- Swipe to **seek**, adjust **brightness**, and control **volume**.
- **Pinch to zoom** and **pan** the video frame.
- **Double‑tap** actions for quick seeking.
- Per‑gesture **sensitivity** tuning.

### 💬 Subtitles
- Support for embedded and external subtitle tracks (SRT, ASS/SSA, WebVTT).
- Customisable **font**, **size**, **bold**, and **background**.
- Optional **styled subtitles** with colours, fonts, and effects.
- Integrates with the system captioning preferences.

### 🎨 Appearance
- **Light, Dark, and System** themes with a **high‑contrast dark** mode.
- **20 accent colours** — 10 curated palettes plus 10 Material colours, chosen from a
  clean swatch grid.
- **Multiple library layouts** — List, Grid, and Compact, each with a live preview.
- A **minimal, consistent settings experience** with Material 3 iconography.

### 🛠 Smart Thumbnails
- Configurable thumbnail strategy — first frame, frame at a chosen position, or a
  smart hybrid mode.
- Built‑in cache management.

## 📲 Installation

1. Download the latest **`GHOST-PLAY.apk`** from the
   [**Releases**](https://github.com/divudon21/GHOST-PLAY/releases) page.
2. Enable **Install from unknown sources** for your browser or file manager.
3. Open the APK and install.

> Requires **Android 7.0 (API 24)** or higher.
